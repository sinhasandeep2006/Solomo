Why “White is Not Right” in Skin Comfort

Most conventional white tissues achieve their snow-like brightness by drenching pulp in chemical chlorine bleaches, dioxins, and synthetic brighteners. Those harsh chemicals break down into tiny dust particles that tickle sensitive baby nostrils and dry out delicate facial skin.

Frequently asked questions

Everything people ask before their first box. More on the FAQ page.

Is unbleached tissue less hygienic than white tissue?expand_more

No. The colour comes from skipping chlorine bleaching, not from skipping cleaning. Bleaching makes tissue whiter, not cleaner.

Will these shed lint on my glasses or my laptop?expand_more

Bamboo fibre is longer than wood pulp, which is what causes shedding. The desk flat box in particular is made for wiping lenses and screens.
